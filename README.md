## Create the impact.

Lightning that snaps. Slashes that hit. Energy that feels alive.

**Shadows AI VFX Editor** is an upcoming project exploring how AI can help Roblox creators bring visual effects to life. This repository is our announcement and community hub—follow along as the project takes shape.

## Release · Coming soon

A release date has not been announced. Development updates and launch information will be shared here.

**Star this repository** to keep it close. For release notifications, select **Watch → Custom → Releases**.

## Seeking VFX editors

We're looking for creators with an eye for **timing, motion, style, and impact** to help shape the project.

Whether your specialty is anime lightning, energy slashes, particles, magic circles, or something completely original, we'd love to see your work.

**[Apply to collaborate →](https://github.com/Juanvpizza/shadows-ai-vfx-editor/issues/new?template=editor-application.yml)**

Include a short introduction, a few examples of your effects, and how you'd like to contribute. Applications are public—only share information you're comfortable posting openly. Contribution arrangements will be discussed before any work begins.

## Help shape what's next

- **Share an idea:** tell us which effects or creative workflows matter to you.
- **Show your style:** submit your best work through an editor application.
- **Follow the launch:** star the repo and watch for release announcements.
